Location:  1372 East Hall Description:  This is a graduate course on convex optimization with a focus on applications in machine learning and statistics. We will discuss several major families of optimization algorithms such as subgradient methods, proximal algorithms and coordinate descent methods. Textbook:  There’s no required textbook. For reference, we will use Convex Optimization Algorithms  by Dimitri P. Bertsekas and Convex Optimization  by Boyd and Vandenberghe. Ctools : You should access the Canvas  class page for this course frequently. It will contain important announcements and posted homework assignments. Homeworks (75%):  There will be 3 equally weighted homework assignments. The problems on the homeworks will be a mix of theoretical and programming problems. There is no “official” programming language for the course. Knowledge of Python/Matlab/R should be sufficient to solve problems. HW 1  (out: Nov 4, due: Nov 11) HW 2  (out: Nov 11, due: Nov 23) HW 3  (out: Nov 25, due: Dec 5) Course project (25%):  Projects will have to be done in pairs (I will permit a group of 3 if the scope of the proposed project is wider). I will post some project ideas but you should feel free to choose a project topic close to your research. Project evaluation will be based on: a 2-pages long project proposal (5%), and a 6-8 pages long final report (20%) in the NIPS 2015 format . Schedule Note: Schedule is subject to change.
