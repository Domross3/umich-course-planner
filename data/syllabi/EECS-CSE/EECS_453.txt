EECS 453: Principles of Machine Learning
Instructor: Qing Qu
Schedule (Fall offering referenced): Mon/Wed 12:00-1:30pm, office hours Wed 3:30-5:00pm, 3 credit hours.
Audience: entry-level ECE machine learning course for senior EE/CE undergrads and junior
master's students outside the SIPML area.

Course content: basic principles of machine learning, covering unsupervised learning
(clustering, mixture models, dimension reduction) and supervised learning (regression,
classification, neural networks and deep learning), emphasizing algorithmic ideas/intuitions
and basic theoretical insights.

Topics: basics of probability, linear algebra, and optimization; regression and linear
prediction; support vector machines and kernel methods; deep neural networks; dimension
reduction (PCA, autoencoders); clustering (K-means, mixture of Gaussians, EM); representation
learning (nonnegative matrix factorization, dictionary learning).

Grading (per search summary): 5 homework assignments (40%), midterm exam (30%), course
project (25%), participation & course evaluation (5%).
