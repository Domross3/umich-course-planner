EECS 587: Parallel Computing
Instructor: Quentin Stout (taught each fall semester)

Course overview: development of programs for parallel computers, including basic concepts
such as speedup, load balancing, latency, system taxonomies, design of algorithms for idealized
models, programming on parallel systems such as shared or distributed memory machines,
networks, grid computing, and performance analysis. Substantial term project.

Languages/tools mentioned: CUDA (GPUs), MPI (distributed memory), OpenMP (shared memory).
Topics: synchronization, load balancing, communication minimization, latency toleration.

Grading (per search summary): written homework ~15%, programs ~40%, project ~45%
(plus possibly a small quiz or two).
