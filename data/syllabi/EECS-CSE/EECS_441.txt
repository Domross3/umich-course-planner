EECS 441 / CSE 441: Mobile App Development for Entrepreneurs
University of Michigan, College of Engineering.
Instructor: Elliot Soloway (Professor).

Description: "Best practices in the software engineering of mobile applications and best
practices of software entrepreneurs in the design, production and marketing of mobile apps."

Course pages found: Course Information (logistics, grades), Syllabus/Schedule (course
policies, dates, deadlines — content hosted off-repo, not machine readable), Student Grades,
Daily Participation Record, Absence Form, Class Recordings, Past Semester Work, Readings
Folder, Guest Speakers, Evaluation Forms (Project Preso Evaluation, Company Preso
Evaluation, Team Evaluation).

Assignments / deliverables listed:
- Assignment #0: Project Analysis
- Elevator Pitch (presented in class)
- Company Presentation (analyze a company of your choice)
- Pitch Your App Idea
- Project Preso #1: App Design / Presentation Research
- Project Preso #2: Customer, Persona, & Mockup / "What problem/need your app will address"
- Project Preso #3: MVP1 Showcase / Target Persona & MVP1
- Project Preso #4: MVP1 Feedback & Solutions / MVP1 Live Demo
- Project Preso #5: MVP2 Showcase / User Feedback & MVP2
- Company Preso: Make a Prediction
- Project Groups: Form a group

The course is a semester-long project course (teams build and iterate a mobile app through
two MVP cycles with pitch/persona/demo/feedback presentations), run in multiple sections
each with their own IA. This course does not teach mobile programming itself; students are
expected to learn the platform on their own (per the department's public course description
found via search but not directly present in the cloned repo, so not otherwise quoted here).
